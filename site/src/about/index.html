Original ValueNet

The BFO-aligned suite re-expresses the original DUL-aligned ValueNet, authored by Stefano De Giorgis. That work is the source tradition and the mapping target. Its licence has not been identified and no rights over it are granted here.

Upstream ontologies

The Basic Formal Ontology and the Common Core Ontologies are vendored under their own licences and cited as references.

Canonical ValueNet IRIs identify ontology entities but are not currently HTTP-dereferenceable. Use the explorer, repository source links, or module downloads to inspect their definitions.
